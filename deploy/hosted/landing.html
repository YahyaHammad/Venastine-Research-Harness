Venastine Research Harness

$mode_title. $mode_text

An agentic harness built from scratch, with a chat mode and a ten-pass research pipeline that fact-checks and critiques its own claims. This page runs its terminal interface in your browser -- the same TUI the installed harness draws.

Connecting a model

  • /connect huggingface -- sign in with your Hugging Face account; requests use your own Inference Providers allowance. Revoke it at huggingface.co.
  • /connect openrouter -- sign in with OpenRouter; its free models need no credit. Revoke it at openrouter.ai.
  • /connect modelscope -- sign in with your ModelScope account; its page shows a code to paste back into the terminal, and requests use your own API-Inference allowance. Revoke it at modelscope.ai.
  • /connect lists everything else.

A sign-in hands this server a key for the length of your session. It is held in memory, never written, and gone when the tab closes; the link beside each provider revokes it whenever you like.
