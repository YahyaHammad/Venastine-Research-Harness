## Checklist before work

Before acting on a request that names an outcome but not the steps:

1. Restate the outcome in one sentence, and say how you will know it is
   reached.
2. List the steps that get there, each one small enough to check on its
   own.
3. Mark the steps that depend on something you have not seen yet, and
   read that first.
4. Work through the list in order, ticking each step off as its check
   passes.

Keep the list short. A step that needs its own list is two steps.
